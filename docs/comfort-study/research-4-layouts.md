Slagcoin general rules: 36-36.5 mm standard spacing for 30 mm buttons (minimum 35-36 mm, nuts are 35-36 mm wide), 29 mm for 24 mm buttons, 36 or 39 mm between rows; stick-to-first-button 59, 63 or 95 mm; many prefer the cluster rotated ~10 deg clockwise.

## 2. Component numbers

Joysticks (slagcoin.com/joystick/attributes_brands.html chart [measured] unless noted)
- Mounting convention: 23-24 mm of shaft between panel top and ball-top underside, 24 mm hole (slagcoin mounting page). Vewlix spacer plate-to-panel 7.5 mm [unverified].
- Handle-to-plate (ball underside to plate top): JLF 32 mm (32.5 mm per paradisearcadeshop.com JLF page); LS-56 31.5; LS-55 33.5; LS-40 29; LS-32 27.5 (stock shaft 30.4 mm, arcadeshock.com); LS-33 25.5. So LS-32 sits ~4.5 mm lower than JLF at the same plate height; LS-56 only 0.5 mm lower.
- Ball tops: 35 mm standard, 30 and 40 mm available; 30 mm reads as a stiffer stick.
- Spring: JLF stock 0.9 lb ~ 400 g; 2 lb ~ LS-32 feel, 4 lb ~ IL Eurojoystick 2, 6 lb ~ IL Universal (arcadexpress.com spring listing; focusattack 2 lb spring page). Slagcoin tension ratings: JLF light, LS-32 medium, LS-40 medium, LS-56 heavy, LS-33 very light, Happ Competition heavy, JLW/Happ Super very heavy.
- Throw/engage (slagcoin ratings): JLF far engage, medium throw; LS-32 medium/short; LS-40 short/short; LS-56 medium/medium; Happ Competition short/medium. No degree figures found.
- Happ/IL body 60 mm deep vs 36-41 mm Japanese; mounted 8-17 mm below panel vs 3-13 mm.

Buttons (slagcoin button chart [measured])
- Sanwa OBSF-30: small switch, "very light", very short engage, plunger face 25 mm, rim 33 mm, snap-in 2.4-4.2 mm panel, body depth 32 mm. ~70 g actuation claim [unverified].
- Seimitsu PS-14-K: small switch, "light", short engage, face 24 mm, snap 1.5-3.4 mm, depth 34 mm. PS-14-G/-KN large switch "medium". PS-14-D 0.2 lbf ~ 90 g [unverified]. Focus Attack lists optional 0.13/0.25/0.50 N Seimitsu springs.
- Button throw 2.5-3 mm for all brands.
- Happ Competition: 28 mm hole, Cherry D44X 75 g switch, "heavy", concave 22 mm face, depth 60 mm.
- Plunger height above panel: not found for any model.

## 3. Findings

1. Rows should arc because the middle finger is longest and the pinky short; index buttons sit 13-15 mm lower than middle in Sega and Vewlix. slagcoin layout page. consensus
2. 30 mm buttons at 36 mm pitch / 24 mm at 29 mm is the practical floor set by rim and nut diameters. slagcoin. consensus
3. Keyboard study: 17-19 mm key pitch performs equally; 16 mm hurt speed and usability in 37 large-handed men. journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0018720812465005 (Pereira 2013). measured
4. Stick-to-button 59 mm suits players who keep elbows in; 95 mm American spacing is "sized for gorillas" and forces two-finger play. slagcoin; SRK thread 154982. common
5. Sega arc preferred over Vewlix for 8-button use because pinky buttons follow the fingertip print; six-button difference is negligible. SRK 154982 (4 of 6 posters). common
6. Stick hand clenches, button hand spreads, so stick should sit level with the bottom row rather than the row average. slagcoin. common
7. Leave empty panel in front and beside controls for palms; arcade panels lack palm room. slagcoin. common
8. Wrist neutral: no extension past the forearm line, elbows ~90 deg, so a thinner box helps; for lap play prefer short boxes and no panel angle. archive.supercombo.gg/t/arcade-stick-ergonomics/58280. minority
9. Japanese parts need less force than Happ; one poster's soreness went away after switching. SRK 58280. single post
10. Sit-down cabinet: panel 75-80 cm high, 14 deg tilt, screen 24 deg; another builder uses 8.5 deg, one flat. forum.arcadecontrols.com/index.php?topic=12523.0. single post
11. LS-56 short throw removed dead-zone hesitation but stiff spring caused forearm burn after 30 min for one player. archive.supercombo.gg/t/seimitsu-ls-56-01-vs-sanwa-jlf-tp-8yt-sk-joystick/137628. single post
12. 24 mm Hit Box action buttons chosen so fingers spread less; effect called negligible. archive.supercombo.gg/t/why-hitbox-uses-24mm-buttons-as-action-button-instead-of-30mm/180540. minority
13. 10 deg clockwise rotation of the cluster matches the resting hand angle. slagcoin. common
14. Seimitsu buttons let you rest fingers on them; Sanwa fire on a brush. archive.supercombo.gg/t/seimitsu-ps-14-kn-v-sanwa-obsf-30-performance/54127. common
